My Fight with Depression During COVID-19

"Sharing my story of fighting uncertainties and insecurities during the COVID-19 pandemic with the UNICEF Italia Team."
TheSgarOfficial June 2020 UNICEF Official Platform

Enduring repeated episodes of depression while navigating internal and external uncertainties was extremely challenging. Creating rays of hope amidst isolation required immense resilience. By opening up about my mental health journey on a global platform with UNICEF, I aimed to offer solidarity to millions experiencing similar struggles.
