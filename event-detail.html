Global Teen Hero 20 Under 20 Award

📍 Soaltee Hotel, Kathmandu, Nepal

Event Overview

National Recognition & Leadership

Recognized as one of the Top 20 Glocal Teen Heroes in Nepal during the prestigious 2017 national awards ceremony held at The Soaltee Hotel in Kathmandu, Nepal.

This honor was awarded in celebration of exceptional youth leadership, initiative, and impactful community activism across the country. Out of hundreds of nationwide nominations, Sagar was selected for his early dedication to social change, public health advocacy, and youth empowerment initiatives.

🏆 Recognition: Top 20 Glocal Teen Hero Finalist
