About Me

Sagar Parajuli

Born on 17 February 1998, Sagar Parajuli is a dedicated public health practitioner from Devchuli-12, Nawalparasi (East), Nepal who advocates for community health empowerment and evidence-based practice.

Master's of Public Health Service Management student at Pokhara University, SRHR Activist, Golden 1000 Days Breastfeeding Counselor, Comprehensive Sexuality Education Trainer, Top 20 Glocal Teen Hero Finalist 2017, and former Province 4 SDGs Co-ordinator at Nepal Youth Network for SDGs (2017–2019).
