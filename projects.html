Health & Community Advocacy

Period Empowerment Project – Comprehensive Menstrual Health Program

"Together Towards for Healthier Periods"
#LoveRespectCelebrateMenstruation #ProudToBleed #MenstrualHealth
Vision & Mission

Initiated with the vision of breaking silence as well as stigma and taboos related to menstruation at the community level, and for promoting healthy and sustainable choices for menstrual health management for every adolescent girl and woman.

Key Achievements
  • Spearheaded two viral social media campaigns: #LoveRespectCelebrateMenstruation and #ProudToBleed.
  • 150 adolescent girls benefited from interactive MHM awareness sessions and practical hands-on training in crafting reusable cloth pads.
Education & Child Welfare

Child Education Support Project

Sponsorship for Child Education
#EduParent #EduDad #EduMom #LetMyEducationAct
Vision & Mission

Towards the Vision of a Child-Friendly Nepal with educational access for every child, the Sponsorship for Education Program was conducted to support needy children financially and materially to help them fulfill their educational dreams.

Key Achievements
  • Provided comprehensive educational sponsorship to more than 50 students across multiple remote and urban districts.
  • Impacted Regions:
    Bajura Tanahun Panchthar Baglung Surkhet
